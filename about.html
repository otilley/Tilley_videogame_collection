About

Hi, I’m Tilley! I'm a 4th year graphic design student at the University of Georgia. Video games have always been an interest of mine, and were part of the reason I discovered my passion for design. I built this collection to showcase all of my favorite games I've played and allow users to navigate through them.

Here I am playing on my 3DS at 8 years old, and while I might not look as crazy anymore, I still have the same enthusiasm as I did back then. If you're interested in other work I've done or games I've played since this photo was taken, check out my links down below.
